# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Boutique web design studios, digital agencies, and independent web consultants who prospect local and regional businesses (HVAC, dental, law firms, home remodeling, medical, specialty dining) with outdated websites.

## Product Purpose

Pitchr turns dated, non-converting websites into booked discovery meetings by automating three linked steps in one unified workflow:
1. Live technical & visual DOM inspection.
2. AI-powered conversion bottleneck diagnosis and personalized pitch synthesis.
3. 1-click multi-provider cold outreach with visual Kanban pipeline tracking.

Success means the user goes from a prospect domain to an authoritative, flaw-specific cold outreach campaign dispatched in under 60 seconds.

## Positioning

Unlike generic scrapers (Apollo, Clay) that output raw contact lists without context, and unlike developer audit tools (Lighthouse) that output dense technical scores meaningless to small business owners, Pitchr bridges the gap: it diagnoses the exact business conversion bottleneck (pinch-to-zoom mobile friction, bloated assets, buried CTA phone numbers) and translates it directly into persuasive client proposals and interactive teaser diagnostic pages (`/audit/[id]`).

## Core Capabilities

- **Diagnostic Scanner**: Live DOM parsing, viewport tag inspection, legacy framework detection, and Google Gemini AI critique.
- **Conversion Bottleneck Prioritizer**: Ranks site weaknesses by sales impact (Mobile Friction > Asset Weight > Design Age > SEO Meta).
- **Outreach Studio**: 4-touch sequential campaign generation with customizable agency tone (Boutique, Direct, Value-first) and direct dispatch via Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, or Authenticated Custom SMTP.
- **Kanban CRM Pipeline**: Drag-and-drop and touch-accessible stage management (`Audited` → `Draft Ready` → `Contacted` → `Replied` → `Converted`).
- **Public Client Teaser**: Safe, read-only diagnostic preview links for prospects with interactive redesign previews.
- **Security & Hardening**: Strict SSRF network protection, sliding-window rate limiters, and server-side secret isolation.

## Confirmed Conventions

- Structural Obsidian dark surfaces (`#191C21`) for high-focus sidebar navigation and cards.
- Clean canvas workspace (`#FAF9F9`) with slate typography.
- Tangerine Orange (`#F97316`) reserved exclusively for primary action triggers and high-value conversion indicators.
- Pure client-side persistence (Local Storage) with zero-leak credential storage and optional Supabase cloud sync.
